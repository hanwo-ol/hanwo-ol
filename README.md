# Hello! I'm Hanwo-ol, a Statistical Analyst & Data Scientist.

I am a researcher at the **Vestibular Lab, Department of Neurology, Biomedical Research Institute, Jeonbuk National University Hospital**, holding an M.S. in Statistics from Jeonbuk National University. My work focuses on clinical statistical analysis (GEE, ANCOVA, MICE, survival analysis) and deep learning modeling (gait and eye-movement biosignals, spatiotemporal forecasting) for vestibular dysfunction and neurological disorders. Alongside formal manuscripts and repositories here on GitHub, I document clinical study notes, server/HPC troubleshooting, and graduate research reflections on my Korean study blog, **[배워서 남주자 (Naver Blog)](https://blog.naver.com/d0njusey0)**.
