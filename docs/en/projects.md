### Behavioral Metric Analysis of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) via Virtual Spatial Navigation (vMWM) [2026.06 - Current]
#### Key Roles
- Built preprocessing pipelines and led statistical analyses for vMWM virtual trajectory data and multivariate behavioral metrics (latency, floating behavior, proximity error, and endpoint search strategies)
- Developed statistical models including Generalized Estimating Equations (GEE), multinomial logistic regression, post-hoc survival analyses (Cox, AFT), and bootstrap validation
- Authored statistical Methods and Results manuscript sections, and performed reproducibility verification and data integrity audits

#### Outcomes
- On progress -- preparing submission to *Alzheimer's Research & Therapy*

---

### Clinical Trial of Standardized *Ginkgo biloba* Extract for Dizziness-Related Disability in Mild Cognitive Impairment [2026.06 - Current]
#### Key Roles
- Formal Clinical & Sensitivity Analysis: Conducted primary ANCOVA, Multiple Imputation by Chained Equations (MICE, $m=20$) for missing data adjustment, tipping-point sensitivity analysis, and 18-point DHI responder analysis
- Clinical Data Curation & Pipeline Development: Preprocessed and curated randomized clinical cohort data ($N=71$) and developed reproducible Python/R analytical pipelines
- Visualization & Manuscript Support: Produced core figures and tables (CONSORT flow diagram, tipping-point heatmaps, DHI score change plots) and reviewed/revised statistical and results sections

#### Outcomes
- On progress -- Submitted and under review at *Phytomedicine* (Manuscript No. PHYMED-D-26-09173) (*Standardized Ginkgo biloba Extract for Dizziness-Related Disability in Mild Cognitive Impairment: A Randomized Placebo-Controlled Trial*)
- Related Domain Study Notes (Naver Blog, Korean): [Dizziness Classification Framework](https://blog.naver.com/d0njusey0/224481478047) · [Migraine Subtypes: EM, CM, VM & Chronic VM](https://blog.naver.com/d0njusey0/224470860382) · [CGRP Study Terminology](https://blog.naver.com/d0njusey0/224470816639)
